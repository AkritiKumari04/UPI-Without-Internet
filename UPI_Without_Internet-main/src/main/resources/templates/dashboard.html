How the demo works

  • Choose sender, receiver, amount, and PIN.
  • Packet is encrypted and injected into nearby offline devices.
  • Each device relays the packet during gossip rounds.
  • A bridge with internet uploads the packet to the backend.
  • Server verifies and settles the transaction exactly once.
